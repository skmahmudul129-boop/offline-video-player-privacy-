Privacy Policy — Offline Video Player

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This policy explains what data Offline Video Player ("the app") accesses and why. Contact: skmahmudul129@gmail.com

1. Media files on your device

The app reads video files stored on your device (internal storage, SD card, OTG) so it can list and play them. This happens entirely on-device — files are never uploaded anywhere by the app itself.

2. Private Vault

Videos you move into the app's Vault are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and stored only on your device. Decryption happens in memory at playback time — no decrypted copy is ever written to disk. We have no way to access your Vault contents; there is no server component.

3. Network features (all opt-in)

If you choose to use them, the app can connect to: SMB/FTP/WebDAV shares on your local network, your Google Drive or Dropbox account (via their official sign-in), Chromecast devices on your Wi-Fi, and nearby devices via Wi-Fi Direct for phone-to-phone file sharing. These connections are made directly between your device and the service/device you chose — the app does not relay or store this traffic on any server we control.

4. Location & Bluetooth permission (Nearby Share only)

Android requires location permission to be granted before an app can scan for nearby Bluetooth/Wi-Fi Direct devices, even though this app does not use your location for anything and does not record, store, or transmit it. This permission is only requested when you open the "কাছের ডিভাইসে শেয়ার" (Nearby Share) feature.

5. Subtitles

If you search for subtitles online, your search query is sent to OpenSubtitles to find matching results. No other personal data is attached to that request beyond what OpenSubtitles' own service requires.

6. Advertising

When your device has an active internet connection, the app may show ads via Google AdMob. These ad networks may collect an advertising identifier and other data as described in Google's Privacy Policy. No ads are shown, and no ad SDK is initialized, while the app is offline. Purchasing the Pro/ad-free upgrade removes ads entirely.

7. In-app purchases

Pro membership purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment details.

8. What we do NOT do

  • We do not run our own backend server that stores your files, viewing history, or personal data.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use analytics/tracking SDKs beyond what the ad network itself requires to serve ads (see §6).

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change and, for material changes, we'll note it in the app's update notes.
